How it works

From headline to options signal in three steps

Each step below shows what it did on a real event: the CrowdStrike outage of July 19, 2024.

  1. 1

    Scan

    Our models watch price, volume, options flow, implied volatility, earnings calendars and news sentiment across U.S. equities and their option chains.

  2. 2

    Score and pick

    Each setup is scored on confidence and risk/reward, and weak or crowded ideas are dropped. For the ones left, the AI picks a strike and expiry whose Greeks fit the expected move.

  3. 3

    Act

    You get a ready-to-read card: ticker, direction, suggested strike and expiry, entry, target, stop, the Greeks, and the reasons behind it. The final call is always yours.

      FAQ

      Questions, answered

      Is AlphOpt financial advice?

      No. AlphOpt provides research signals and educational information. It does not know your finances, goals or risk tolerance. Always do your own research, and consider speaking with a licensed advisor before investing.

      Do I need to understand options to use it?

      No. Every card explains the contract in plain language: what you pay, the most you can lose, and what the stock needs to do for the trade to work. The Greeks lab lets you try “what if the stock drops?” or “what if I hold a week?” before you buy.

      Are the replays real trades?

      The events and stock prices are real: actual daily closes, volumes and ranges from the time. The trades are replays: we applied AlphOpt's rules (+50% target, −35% stop, five sessions at most, checked at each close) to those events. AlphOpt had not launched yet and did not publish these signals. Option premiums and Greeks are model estimates, not recorded quotes. Losses stay in, so you can judge the rules on all of it.

      How does the AI choose signals?

      Models trained on historical market data score each setup using technical patterns, options-market activity, volatility, and news and earnings context. Only setups that clear confidence and risk/reward thresholds are published, each with the top factors behind it.

      Does AlphOpt place trades for me?

      No. AlphOpt never touches your brokerage account. You read the signal and decide whether to act on it with your own broker.

      Which markets are covered?

      At launch, AlphOpt covers U.S.-listed stocks and ETFs and their listed options. More markets are on the roadmap.
