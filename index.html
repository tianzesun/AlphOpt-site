Income strategies

Covered calls: earn income from stocks you already own

AlphOpt's focus is option-income strategies. The AI picks which stocks to use, which strike and which expiry, aiming for steady premium income while keeping risk visible.

Covered call

You own 100 shares and sell someone the right to buy them at a higher price (the strike) by a set date. You're paid a premium up front, and you keep it no matter what.

You get
Premium income every month, on top of any dividends
You give up
Gains above the strike. If the stock rallies past it, your shares are sold at the strike
Main risk
You still own the stock. If it falls, the premium only cushions the loss

Cash-secured put

You set aside cash and agree to buy a stock you like at a lower price (the strike). You're paid a premium for making that promise.

You get
Premium income, and the chance to buy shares below today's price
You give up
Gains beyond the premium if the stock rises
Main risk
If the stock drops below the strike, you must buy it at the strike anyway

    FAQ

    Questions, answered

    Is AlphOpt financial advice?

    No. AlphOpt provides research signals and educational information. It does not know your finances, goals or risk tolerance. Always do your own research, and consider speaking with a licensed advisor before investing.

    Are covered calls safer than a GIC?

    No. A GIC guarantees your principal and is CDIC-insured within limits. A covered call is stock ownership plus extra income: it can earn more than a GIC in flat or gently rising markets, but if the stock falls you lose money, cushioned only by the premium you collected. Think of it as a way to earn more from stocks you're comfortable owning, not as a replacement for guaranteed savings.

    Do I need to understand options to use it?

    No. You can stick to stock signals. When you're ready for options, every options card explains the contract in plain language: what you pay, the most you can lose, and what the stock needs to do for the trade to work.

    How does the AI choose signals?

    Models trained on historical market data score each setup using technical patterns, options-market activity, volatility, and news and earnings context. Only setups that clear confidence and risk/reward thresholds are published, each with the top factors behind it.

    Does AlphOpt place trades for me?

    No. AlphOpt never touches your brokerage account. You read the signal and decide whether to act on it with your own broker.

    Which markets are covered?

    At launch, AlphOpt covers U.S.-listed stocks and ETFs and their listed options. More markets are on the roadmap.
