Privacy policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

AlphOpt (“we”, “us”) is an options-education project. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We aim to follow Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and Canada’s Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL).

What we collect

  • Waitlist: your email address, your consent to receive launch emails, the language you were using and the time you signed up. That is all we ask for.
  • Your browser: your language choice is saved in your browser’s local storage so the site remembers it. It stays on your device and is never sent to us.
  • Hosting: this site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses to run and secure its service. We do not receive or use those logs. See GitHub’s privacy statement for details.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics, tracking cookies or social-media pixels.

How we use it

We use your email address only to tell you about AlphOpt’s launch. We do not sell, rent or trade your information, and we do not share it with anyone for their own marketing.

Where it is stored

Sign-ups are processed and stored for us by Formspree, Inc., a service provider in the United States. Information stored in the United States may be accessed by courts, law-enforcement and national-security authorities there under U.S. law.

How long we keep it

We keep your email address until we have sent AlphOpt’s launch updates, or until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it, whichever comes first. Then we delete it.

Your choices and rights

  • You can withdraw your consent at any time, using the unsubscribe link in any email we send or by contacting us.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. We will respond within 30 days.
  • If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada at priv.gc.ca.

Age

AlphOpt is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has joined the waitlist, contact us and we will delete their information.

Security

This site is served only over HTTPS, and we use reasonable safeguards to protect the information we hold. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here and update the date above. If a change materially affects how we use your information, we will tell people on the waitlist by email.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email contact@aydenait.com.

This policy is available in English and Chinese. If the two versions differ, the English version applies.

隐私政策

最后更新：2026年11月16日

AlphOpt（“我们”）是一个期权教育项目。本政策说明我们通过本网站收集哪些个人信息、收集的原因，以及你拥有的选择。我们力求遵守加拿大《个人信息保护和电子文件法》（PIPEDA）和加拿大反垃圾邮件法（CASL）。

我们收集的信息

  • 候补名单：你的电子邮箱地址、你同意接收上线邮件的记录、你当时使用的语言以及注册时间。我们只要求这些信息。
  • 你的浏览器：你的语言选择保存在浏览器的本地存储中，以便网站记住它。它只保存在你的设备上，不会发送给我们。
  • 网站托管：本网站托管在 GitHub Pages 上。GitHub 可能会为运营和保护其服务而记录 IP 地址等技术信息。我们不会接收或使用这些日志。详情请参阅 GitHub 的隐私声明。
  • 我们不使用广告、分析工具、跟踪 Cookie 或社交媒体像素。

我们如何使用这些信息

我们只会用你的电子邮箱地址通知你 AlphOpt 上线的消息。我们不会出售、出租或交易你的信息，也不会与任何人分享用于其自身营销。

信息存储在哪里

注册信息由美国服务商 Formspree, Inc. 代我们处理和存储。存储在美国的信息可能会依照美国法律被当地法院、执法机构和国家安全机构访问。

我们保留信息多长时间

我们会保留你的电子邮箱地址，直到我们发送完 AlphOpt 的上线消息，或你退订、要求我们删除为止，以先发生者为准。之后我们会将其删除。

你的选择和权利

  • 你可以随时撤回同意：点击我们任何邮件中的退订链接，或与我们联系。
  • 你可以要求查看、更正或删除我们持有的你的个人信息。我们会在 30 天内回复。
  • 如果你对我们的回复不满意，可以向加拿大隐私专员办公室投诉：priv.gc.ca。

年龄

AlphOpt 面向成年人。我们不会在知情的情况下收集 18 岁以下人士的信息。如果你认为有未成年人加入了候补名单，请与我们联系，我们会删除其信息。

安全

本网站仅通过 HTTPS 提供，我们采取合理的保护措施保护所持有的信息。但任何传输或存储方式都无法做到绝对安全。

本政策的变更

如果我们修改本政策，会在此处发布新版本并更新上方日期。如果变更会对我们使用你的信息的方式产生重大影响，我们会通过电子邮件通知候补名单上的用户。

联系我们

如有隐私问题或请求，请发送电子邮件至 contact@aydenait.com。
